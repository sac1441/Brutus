Privacy Policy for Brutus

Last updated: July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Brutus ("the app," "we," "us") handles information when you use our mobile game. We built Brutus as an independent developer and take a minimal-data approach: the app itself does not require account creation and does not collect personal information directly.

Information Collected by Third-Party Services

Brutus uses Google AdMob to display rewarded video advertisements (for example, an optional ad you can watch to revive after falling). AdMob may collect and process certain information to serve and measure ads, which can include:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and general location (city/region level)
  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • App interaction data related to ad views and clicks

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly. You can learn more about how Google collects and uses this data, and manage your ad personalization settings, at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Ads Settings

Data We Do Not Collect

Brutus does not require you to create an account, and we do not directly collect your name, email address, contact list, photos, or other personal files. We do not sell any data to third parties.

In-Game Data

Your in-game progress (such as collected eggs, flowers, and score) is stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to us and is not linked to your identity.

Children's Privacy

Brutus is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features or services that affect data handling. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
